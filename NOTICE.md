# Third-party sources

The experiment datasets are available from the following source pages. Exact input identities and preparation steps are listed in the [data guide](data/README.md).

- [MNIST](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/hojjatk/mnist-dataset)
- [Fashion-MNIST](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/zalando-research/fashionmnist)
- [Alzheimer disease](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rabieelkharoua/alzheimers-disease-dataset)
- [Brain Tumor MRI](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/masoudnickparvar/brain-tumor-mri-dataset)

The MRI workflow uses torchvision's ResNet-18 `IMAGENET1K_V1` weights and transforms. The datasets, weights and installed dependencies are subject to their respective terms.
